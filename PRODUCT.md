# PRODUCT — G&G Caulking Service LLC

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (no framework, no tracking), hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page site)

## Users
- **First:** the owner, Orlando Gonzalez, who sees the link on his phone inside Facebook's in-app browser. The site has to win him over within one screen.
- **Then:** property owners, general contractors and property managers in the Essex County, NJ contractor network (inferred from the Facebook group the work was posted in) who need concrete joints and seams sealed and want a free estimate.

## Purpose
Give a 24/7 caulking crew with no website and no published phone number a place to send people, and a way for them to ask for a free estimate.

## Positioning
A blue-collar crew that works around the clock. Their posted work (Facebook group video, 2026-10-18) shows a new commercial lot: a concrete slab with saw-cut joints, worked at night and in daylight, with a sealant bucket on site.

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact today: Facebook only. The work was posted from Orlando's personal profile (https://facebook.com/profile.php?id=100034139794307). No business page, phone, email or booking link exists.
- The estimate form puts together a message. With no phone number yet, it copies the message and opens Facebook. Once a phone number is set (one constant in the page), the form switches to a pre-filled text message and the header shows the number.
- No address or service area is published. "Essex County, NJ" is **inferred** from the group and is not stated on the page.

## Brand commitments
- Name exactly as written: "G&G Caulking Service LLC".
- No logo and no brand colors exist. The palette comes from their own footage: concrete grey ground, layout-marking orange (#ff5b14), chalk-line blue for focus, sealant grey, and twilight navy for the night section (**inferred** from the footage and the trade).
- Tone: work-first, plain and short. "We" voice, because they write "We work 24/7".

## Evidence on hand
- Caption (2026-10-18): "We work 24/7 contact us to get a free estimate." #construction #bluecollar
- A 30-second job-site video. Five stills were taken from it (sealed joint close-up, the slab, the lot behind caution tape, the building front, the slab at night).
- No reviews, prices, licensing or insurance claims, or years in business.

## Principles
1. Facts only: no invented services, phone number, area, licensing or reviews.
2. Phone first: everything is designed at 360–430px wide first.
3. One contact path, done well: a free estimate, sent where Orlando will actually see it.
